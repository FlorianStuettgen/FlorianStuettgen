# Florian Stuettgen

I build software for **project controls and data analysis**, connecting reported results to the evidence, rules, and decisions behind them.

My focus is the whole path: define the business question, design the data contracts and controls, make the result understandable, and leave a verification trail another person can follow.

## Selected work

### EQ-Proof · Check the forecast

**Does the reported position agree with the records beneath it?** Checks cost forecasts, change, and risk alongside schedule exports, tracing failed controls to source records. The synthetic walkthrough follows an $11M discrepancy to its source finding, then checks corrected input snapshots.

[Open the interactive demo](https://florianstuettgen.github.io/EQ-Proof/) · [Follow the worked case](https://github.com/FlorianStuettgen/EQ-Proof/blob/main/docs/SHOWCASE.md) · [Review the code](https://github.com/FlorianStuettgen/EQ-Proof)

### Schrödinger’s Close · Follow the review decision

**What changed between reporting periods, and why is the close blocked?** The synthetic showcase connects forecast movements, supporting evidence, remediation history, and the recorded close decision. It demonstrates how an analytical finding stays attached to the review process.

**Private source · synthetic showcase.** The public viewer has no project-file intake or live project connection.

[Explore the close review](https://florianstuettgen.github.io/schroedingers-close-demo/) · [Read the demonstration guide](https://github.com/FlorianStuettgen/schroedingers-close-demo)

### Query Cartographer · Understand reporting logic

**What is this SQL doing, and what could change its meaning?** A browser workbench for inspecting SQL structure, approximate lineage, and diagnostics before proposing a change. Findings and suggested repairs need validation against the intended data and business rules.

**Private source · browser demonstration.** Static analysis does not establish business correctness or prove a rewrite equivalent.

[Explore the SQL workbench](https://florianstuettgen.github.io/query-cartographer-demo/) · [Read the demonstration guide](https://github.com/FlorianStuettgen/query-cartographer-demo)

### SOC_Replay · Make results reproducible

**Can another analyst reproduce the result—and verify that a faster implementation preserved it?** A security-telemetry lab that compares indexed detection with a full-scan reference and produces verifiable evidence bundles. It demonstrates engineering practices relevant to reliable analytical pipelines: typed inputs, explicit rules, equivalence checks, and reproducible outputs.

[Inspect a completed run](https://github.com/FlorianStuettgen/SOC_Replay/blob/main/reference/network-scan/report.md) · [Review the code](https://github.com/FlorianStuettgen/SOC_Replay)

## How I approach the work

- **Start with the decision.** Make the question, assumptions, and consequences explicit.
- **Keep evidence attached.** Preserve source identity and the reasoning behind each result.
- **Make the boundaries visible.** Separate demonstrated behavior, unsupported cases, and future work.

These are independent projects. Demonstration figures are synthetic; they are not claims of delivered savings or production adoption.
